## IFRC

**To:** secretariat@ifrc.org

**Subject:** Non-commercial use of the 2020 First Aid Guidelines in a free disaster app

Dear IFRC team,

My name is Erkam Bilal Bal. I am building Lifeline, a free and open-source disaster
assistant, for the Firebase #BuildHybridAI 2026 hackathon. The app gives people in
earthquake-prone regions trustworthy guidance when mobile networks fail.

The International First Aid, Resuscitation, and Education Guidelines 2020 state that
copies may be made for non-commercial use if the source is acknowledged, and that
the IFRC would appreciate details of such use. I am writing to share those details.

I plan to summarize relevant guidance as short steps in my own words, credit the
IFRC on every card, and avoid all emblems and logos. The app will have no commercial
income.

If you have any concerns, or if you recommend a more suitable resource, I would be
glad to hear from you.

Project: https://github.com/erkambbb/lifeline-hybrid-ai

Kind regards,
Erkam Bilal Bal
